Geoffrey Hodson

Geoffrey Hodson

GEOFFREY HODSON was born and educated in England. During the First World War he served with distinction in the British Army and afterwards became actively interested in Theosophical studies. He carried out occult research in collaboration with doctors, physicists, anthropologists and archeologists.

For over 60 years Mr. Hodson lectured for the Theosophical Society, speaking in America, England, Europe, South Africa, India, New Zealand, Australia and the Far East. He was the author of about 40 books, dealing with topics such as meditation, the spiritual life, health and disease, and the life and powers of the occultist.

He carried out special and first-hand investigations of fairies and angelic evolution. Mr. Hodson was 4 times Director of Studies at the School of the Wisdom at the International Headquarters of the Theosophical Society, Adyar, Chennai.

The Lesser Gods

The Lesser Gods are the Elementals, the Nature Spirits, most closely associated with the four classical elements. They are the builders, the unseen workforce of the natural world — constructing the forms of minerals, plants, animals, and weather; directing the growth of roots and crystals; guiding the movement of water and flame.

Pleasure is gained by Nature Spirits in moving, dancing, and flying along the lines of force within the fields of ether. Their life is one of thought-free, spontaneous play with the currents of creative energy.

Reference: Fairies at Work and at Play by Geoffrey Hodson, 1925

Air

Sylphs and Fairies

The elementals of air — swift, light, and mercurial. Associated with wind, breath, thought, and the upper atmosphere. Their forms often suggest feathers, wings, or streaming ribbons of light.

Fairy Pancakes

In his 1969 book Passport to Magonia: From Folklore to Flying Saucers, Jacques Vallee, a French computer scientist and ufologist, poses a theory that perhaps the alien encounters of today are the fairy and other folklore encounters of the past.

Hodson says the etheric form of Nature Spirits is governed by at least 3 influences: the archetype (the idea of every form), the element, and human habits — the dress and the popular thought concerning the appearance of fairy people of particular times and places.

The Gods of Gold

There is a hierarchy of Gods, the chord of whose nature is identical with that of gold. They may be thought of as the divine idea of gold manifest as an Order of living beings. Members of this hierarchy are drawn by vibratory affinity into the streams of gold force which are constantly descending from the creative Source into the physical world.

Their presence and assistance intensify the component frequencies and thereby augment the form-producing power of the Word-force. Thus, part of the function of the Gods of gold is to assist in the process of the production of physical substances and forms.

An Angelic Order exists which has evolved through the insect-branch of Nature. Just as Monads manifested through other facets of divine ideation are guarded and aided by their evolutionary seniors, so are those who, when their Rays first touch the physical world, find their embodiment as thousands of tiny insects.

The Greater Gods

PLATE 20

The Greater Gods are the architects — the Archangels, Devas, Angels, and Sephiroth. In the present Mahamanvantara their will does not become markedly differentiated from the One Will. The human sense of separated personality is almost entirely absent in them.

Their path of evolution does not deeply penetrate the physical world and leads from instinctive to self-conscious co-operation with the One Will.

Their Active Role in Human Life

Theosophical teaching holds that every human being has a guardian angel — a deva whose specific function is to accompany that soul through its incarnations, holding the pattern of its higher self and providing an invisible source of protection, inspiration, and guidance.

This is not metaphor. It is a structural feature of human existence that has been attested across virtually every spiritual tradition in the world.

"True music is a temporary, physical expression of the sound of the ever-uttered 'Word'. Throughout creative Day, the Great Breath is breathed forth as music and received back as music."

Angelic Healing

PLATE 24

These pictures portray three different types of angels engaged in their healing ministry. The general method is first to direct streams of purifying energy into and through the aura of the sufferer, in order to disperse congestions in the etheric and emotional bodies.

They then re-attune the chord of "Word" of the individual, usually dissonant in the psyche during ill-health, and try to restore the harmonious and rhythmic flow of the inner Life-forces throughout the mental, emotional and physical nature.

Finally, they invoke divine healing power from its Source into the patients.

"Such ministrations are far more effective when man consciously invokes and cooperates with the healing angels."

The World Mother

PLATE 28 — THE MIRACLE OF BIRTH

During the descent into incarnation of each life cycle, angels are present and assist in the dual process of the construction of the new bodies — mental, emotional, etheric, and physical — and the induction into them of the reincarnating ego.

Universal substance, or prakriti, is the womb wherein all worlds gestate, from which all are born and to which all return. The true World Mother is this primary substance of a universe when first differentiated from the root of matter or mulaprakriti; therein reside the seeds of all living things and the powers of conservation and reproduction.

The planetary World Mother is beyond all limitation of form. No picture can truly represent Her.

Rediscovering the Hidden Kingdoms

Annie Besant spoke of the coming Aquarian age as a period in which the relationship between the human and deva kingdoms would be consciously restored — not as the fitful, uncertain perceptions of gifted individuals, but as a recognized reality integrated into the fabric of civilization.

"The Aquarian age will see a new relationship between humanity and the deva kingdom — one of conscious cooperation, mutual recognition, and shared service to the evolving life of the planet."

These kingdoms have not gone away. They have been present throughout every epoch of human history, working ceaselessly in the natural world, responsive to human attention and intention. What has changed is our willingness — or ability — to perceive them.

How to Begin

  • 🌿 Spend Time in Nature — The nature spirits are most perceptible to a still, attentive, and genuinely open consciousness. Sit by water. Walk in woods.
  • ✦ Open Heart and Mind — Approach these kingdoms with genuine openness, without demand, and with the expectation of encountering something real.
  • ✧ Send Beauty Outward — A genuinely loving, generous thought sent toward the nature of a place or the being of a tree is not wasted.
